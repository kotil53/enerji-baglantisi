⚡ ENERJİ BAĞLANTISI

Power flows from the source but never reaches the bulb. Rotate the wires one by one, reconnect the broken circuit and light up every bulb.

The rule is simple and hard to put down: every tap rotates one piece. Don't waste moves — your stars depend on how few you use.

▸ HOW TO PLAY
• Build a path from the source (⚡) to the bulbs (💡)
• Tap pieces to rotate them
• Locked pieces (🔒) never move — route the solution around them
• The level is complete when every bulb is lit

▸ FEATURES
• Levels — 200 levels, the same for every player
• Daily — seven fresh puzzles every day, the same for everyone
• Endless — puzzles that never run out and keep getting harder; unlocks after you finish Levels
• Four colors of energy: send each to its own bulb and keep them apart
• Fan bulb: fill every socket in the center to spin the fan
• Up to 3 stars per level — solve it in few moves and without hints
• Every level is timed, and you have lives that refill over time
• Stuck? A hint fixes up to two wires. You get 3 free hints every day; after that, watch a short ad for more
• Works offline — no internet needed
• Relaxing ambient music, haptic feedback and a clean dark design
• Available in English, Türkçe, Español and العربية
• No account, no sign-in, no registration

▸ REMINDERS
With your permission, the game can send a gentle reminder when your daily puzzle is ready or you haven't played for a while. You can turn it off in Settings.

The game is free and shows ads: a banner at the bottom, short ads between levels, and optional ads that give you extra hints and lives.

For a short break or a long sit-down: rotate one more wire.
